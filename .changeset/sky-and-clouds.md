Sky and clouds: `sky` draws a gradient with an optional sun or crescent moon (with a soft glow) and stars; `clouds` adds rows of puffy clouds or thin streaks at different depths, repeating horizontally and drifting with parallax. Palettes gain `sun` and `cloud` colours, and there are two new presets, `summer` and `starlight`. Rendered layers are now lists of shapes (`kind`, `key`, `shapes`) so skies and clouds can carry gradients, glows and opacity; ground-only output is unchanged.
